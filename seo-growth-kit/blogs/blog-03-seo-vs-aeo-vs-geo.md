# SEO vs AEO vs GEO: What Indian D2C Brands Actually Need in 2026

**Short answer: you need all three, in that order, weighted roughly 50/30/20 for most Indian D2C brands — SEO remains the foundation, AEO captures the fastest-growing answer surfaces, and GEO builds the brand entity that AI assistants recommend.** Brands that treat them as competing fads waste budget; brands that layer them correctly compound visibility across every surface buyers use.

## The 60-second definitions

| Discipline | Optimises for | Primary surfaces | Core question it answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SEO** (Search Engine Optimization) | Rankings in classic search results | Google/Bing organic listings | "Can buyers find my pages?" |
| **AEO** (Answer Engine Optimization) | Extractable answers | Featured snippets, People Also Ask, Google AI Overviews | "Do machines quote my answers?" |
| **GEO** (Generative Engine Optimization) | Brand/entity recommendations | ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot | "Do machines recommend my brand?" |

## Why Indian D2C can't skip any of the three

1. **SEO still carries the majority of commercial search.** Category, product and "cost" queries with buying intent still end in clicks. A D2C brand with weak technical SEO leaks the highest-intent traffic to marketplaces and competitors.
2. **AEO is where the SERP is heading.** Question and comparison queries increasingly surface AI Overviews and snippets. The brand whose answer gets extracted wins the impression even before the click.
3. **GEO decides the zero-click future.** A growing set of buyers now asks an assistant — "best serum for oily skin under ₹800", "reliable Shopify agency in India" — and buys from the recommendation. If your entity is invisible or inconsistent, you're not in that conversation at all.

## What each discipline requires (effort breakdown)

| Requirement | SEO | AEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|---|
| Technical health (speed, indexability, schema) | ✔ essential | ✔ essential | ✔ essential |
| Keyword-mapped pages | ✔ | ✔ (question-mapped) | ✔ (entity-mapped) |
| Named authors & E-E-A-T | Helpful | Important | Critical |
| Original data / case studies | Helpful | Strong | Critical |
| Consistent NAP & business facts | Local SEO only | Important | Critical |
| Third-party mentions & reviews | Links value | Corroboration value | Primary value |
| Answer-first formatting | Neutral | Essential | Strong |

## The budget split we recommend (and why)

For an Indian D2C brand from zero:

- **Days 1–90: ~50% SEO, ~30% AEO, ~20% GEO.** Fix technical debt, ship the core 20–30 question-led articles with schema, clean up entity facts, start reviews and two directory profiles.
- **Days 90–180: shift toward ~35/35/30.** As foundational content indexes, expand answer coverage and push citation assets (data reports, comparisons, listicles).
- **Always:** paid media runs in parallel — organic is a compounding asset, not this quarter's revenue.

## Three mistakes that waste the entire budget

1. **Publishing 100 thin AI-written posts.** None become citable; several may be filtered as scaled low-value content. Twenty complete, expert-reviewed answers beat a hundred summaries.
2. **Doing GEO without SEO.** A site a crawler can't parse or trusts won't get cited anywhere. Sequence matters.
3. **Ignoring the entity layer.** We audit brands whose address differs across their own site, Google Business Profile and Justdial — then wonder why AI assistants recommend a competitor. Consistency is the cheapest high-impact work in this entire field.

## How to measure each layer honestly

- **SEO:** clicks, impressions, average position, conversions by landing page (Search Console + GA4 — measured).
- **AEO:** featured snippet and PAA ownership for your question set; AI Overview presence on priority queries (weekly manual checks — measured); citation share (monthly sampling — proxy).
- **GEO:** fixed-prompt panels across ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini ("best __ for __ India") — mention rate trend (proxy); branded search volume growth (measured); review velocity and ratings (measured).

Don't let any agency sell you "AI ranking scores" as precise analytics — this field has direction and share, not gospel numbers. Anyone promising guarantees is guessing loudly.

## FAQs

### Is SEO dead because of AI?
No. AI answer engines are built on top of search indexes. Weakened SEO = weakened AEO and GEO. What's dead is *unstructured, anonymous, generic* content — which was dying anyway.

### Should a small D2C brand start with AEO or SEO?
SEO foundations first (they take longest to compound), AEO formatting built in from day one — it's a writing discipline, not a separate project. GEO entity work (NAP, profiles, reviews) starts immediately because it's cheap and slow-burning.

### How is GEO different from link building?
Link building asks "does the link pass authority?". GEO asks "would a credible third party describing us help an AI confidently recommend us?" A listicle feature with context about your specialisation can be worth more than ten bare directory links.

### What does an AEO/GEO retainer actually involve?
Question mapping, answer-first content production, schema engineering, entity cleanup, citation/digital-PR outreach, review systems, and monthly citation tracking. It's SEO plus a trust-engineering layer — not a mysterious new product.

## Conclusion

SEO earns the right to be seen. AEO earns the right to be quoted. GEO earns the right to be recommended. Indian D2C brands that stack all three — in the right sequence — will be findable, quotable and recommendable while their competitors argue about which acronym is a fad.
