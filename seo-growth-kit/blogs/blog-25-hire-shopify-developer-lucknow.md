# Hiring a Shopify Developer in Lucknow: What to Check & What It Should Cost

**A good Shopify hire in Lucknow costs roughly ₹20,000–₹50,000 (capable freelancer, template build), ₹50,000–₹1,50,000 (agency, customised + conversion-ready), and more for custom app/Plus work — and the price gap is almost always explained by what's *missing* from the cheaper quote: tracking, speed discipline, catalogue structure and post-launch accountability.** This guide gives you the verification checklist that separates real Shopify developers from theme-installers with a logo.

## First, be clear on what you're buying

"Shopify developer" covers three very different jobs:

| You need | You're hiring | Typical Lucknow cost |
|---|---|---|
| A store that launches and works | Setup specialist (theme config, payments, shipping) | ₹15,000–₹50,000 |
| A store that *sells* | Growth developer (customisation, speed, CRO, tracking, integrations) | ₹50,000–₹1,50,000 |
| Things no theme can do | App/custom developer (Liquid deep-work, APIs, checkout extensions, Plus) | ₹1,50,000+ / dedicated contracts |

Most disappointed buyers hired category 1 while expecting category 2.

## The 8-point verification checklist

**1. Two live stores, opened on your phone.** Not screenshots, not Behance mockups — real URLs, tested on your device, on mobile data. Judge speed with your thumb, not their pitch.

**2. Speed proof.** Ask them to run their own live store through PageSpeed Insights in front of you. Mobile scores in the red on their *own* work tell you everything.

**3. Tracking included or not?** Ask directly: "Do you set up GA4, Meta pixel and Conversions API, and verify test events before handover?" If it's an add-on they shrug about, your ads will launch blind.

**4. Catalogue thinking.** Ask how they'd structure 200 SKUs across collections for SEO and UX. Real developers talk architecture; installers talk colours.

**5. Written scope with named deliverables.** "Theme customisation, 100 products migrated, payments+COD config, GA4+Meta verified, redirect map, 2 rounds of revisions, 30-day support" — countable lines beat "complete Shopify website".

**6. Who actually writes the code?** In agencies, ask about the specific developer; with freelancers, ask about capacity during your launch window. The gap between salesperson and coder is where timelines quietly stretch.

**7. Post-launch terms.** A 30-day fix window in writing is professional hygiene. "Support as per requirement" means per-invoice.

**8. India-commerce fluency.** COD flows, UPI-first checkout ordering, courier integrations, GST invoicing, marketplace sync — Lucknow buyers *should* know these cold. It's a fair local-interview question.

## Freelancer vs agency: the honest matrix

| | Freelancer | Agency |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Lower | Higher |
| Speed (simple builds) | Often faster | Slower, more process |
| Depth (custom work, integrations) | Varies wildly | Team bench, usually deeper |
| Continuity risk | One person's availability = your timeline | Team coverage |
| CRO/growth thinking | Rarely included | Should be included (verify) |
| Best for | Clean builds, tight budgets, simple catalogues | Revenue-critical launches, custom needs, ongoing growth |

The trap isn't either option — it's hiring a freelancer at agency expectations or an agency budget for freelancer scope.

## What a fair quote includes (walk away if it doesn't)

- Itemised scope (design, catalogue, payments, shipping, tracking, QA, redirects if migrating)
- Mobile-speed budget ("loads under ~3s on 4G" in writing)
- Tracking verification as a deliverable, not a favour
- A staging/preview process (nobody edits your live store)
- Timeline with a launch date and your side's data deadlines
- Post-launch support window

## Interview questions that expose weak work fast

1. "Show me your two best live stores — on my phone."
2. "What made those stores fast — what did you specifically do?"
3. "How will you verify my ads will track orders before launch?"
4. "What's your QA checklist before handover?" (The good ones have a literal checklist.)
5. "What will you need from me, and by when?" (Professionals manage your side of the timeline.)

## FAQs

### How much does a Shopify developer charge in Lucknow?
Template-based builds with capable freelancers run roughly ₹20,000–₹50,000; customised, conversion-ready agency builds typically ₹50,000–₹1,50,000; complex custom/app/Plus work prices beyond that. Always compare scope lines, not totals.

### Is it safe to hire a freelancer for my store?
Yes, with verification: live work, written scope, your-ownership of the store and domain, and a defined support window. The failure mode is scope ambiguity, not freelancing itself.

### Should I pay for a custom design?
Usually not at launch. A fast premium theme, customised properly, beats a slow custom design on every metric that matters. Custom design earns its price when your brand genuinely needs differentiated experience *and* you have traffic to prove the need.

### What documents/access should I never hand over?
Never give your Shopify store ownership, domain registrar, or payment gateway credentials to sit under anyone else's account. Staff accounts and collaborator access exist precisely so you keep ownership while granting work access.

### How long does a professional Shopify build take?
Straightforward builds: 2–4 weeks. Customised or migrated stores: 4–8 weeks. The biggest schedule killer is always client-side data (catalogue, photos, content) — ask the developer to give you a data checklist on day one.

Hiring a Shopify developer in Lucknow is a verification exercise: live stores, speed proof, tracking as a deliverable, written scope, your ownership. Pay fair prices for real scope and you'll buy an asset; pay bargain prices for vague scope and you'll buy a rebuild next year.
