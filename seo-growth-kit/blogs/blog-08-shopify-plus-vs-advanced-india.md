# Shopify Plus vs Shopify Advanced: Which Plan Fits Your Indian D2C Brand?

**Short answer: Advanced (or even Basic) is the right answer until roughly ₹50L–₹1Cr+ in monthly online revenue or until you specifically need checkout customisation, high-volume automation, or multi-store/market operations — because Shopify Plus in India costs multiple lakhs per month, and that money outperforms almost anywhere else until scale makes its features unavoidable.**

Here's the honest breakdown we give clients on plan-selection calls — including the features people pay for and never use.

## The cost reality (India)

|---|---|---|

## What actually differs in day-to-day operations

1. **Checkout control.** The single real technical difference that matters. On Plus you can build custom checkout experiences — post-purchase upsells, B2B fields, regional payment logic. On Advanced you work within standard checkout. For most sub-₹1Cr/month brands, standard checkout converts fine and apps cover 90% of the wishlist.
2. **Automation at volume.** Flow + Launchpad shine when you run frequent flash sales, huge SKU counts, or complex omnichannel ops. At 200 orders a day, Advanced + a few apps does the same job.
3. **B2B and org structure.** Multiple brands/countries/stores under one roof, wholesale price lists, company accounts — genuinely Plus territory.
4. **Support and partnership depth.** A named success team matters when downtime costs lakhs per hour, not when it costs a few orders.

## The decision framework we use

**Choose/stay on Advanced when:**
- Monthly online revenue is under ~₹50L
- Your bottleneck is traffic, conversion rate or operations — not platform limits (it almost always is)
- You'd fund Plus by cutting ads or CRO — the exact spend that grows revenue

**Move to Plus when any of these is true:**
- Checkout experiments are a proven revenue lever you've exhausted alternatives for
- You operate multiple stores/brands/markets needing central management
- Flash-sale frequency (multi-tonne launches) needs Launchpad-grade orchestration
- B2B/wholesale is a real channel needing company accounts and price lists
- Platform fees are already <2–3% of revenue and engineering leverage will compound it

## Where the money goes further (the honest math)

₹2L/month buys, on Advanced: meaningful Meta/Google spend, a creative testing budget, a CRO experiment program, or WhatsApp/retention automation — each of which has moved more revenue for our clients at sub-₹1Cr scale than any plan upgrade. Plus is not a growth strategy; it's infrastructure for a growth strategy that already works.

**The rare exception:** if your model *is* high-frequency drops or checkout-dependent monetisation, Plus features are the product. Then upgrade early and build on it.

## Migration note

Upgrading is technically simple (same admin), but checkout extensibility, Flow rebuilds and Launchpad events still need engineering planning — budget implementation time, not just subscription cost.

## FAQs

### How much does Shopify Plus cost in India?
Plus is priced in USD, typically starting around $2,000+/month with variable platform fees tied to volume — roughly ₹2L+/month all-in for most Indian brands. Confirm current terms with Shopify or a partner, as pricing varies by region and contract.

### Can I get checkout upsells without Shopify Plus?
Partially — app-based post-purchase and cart upsells work on standard plans within checkout extensibility limits. Plus gives deeper, native control. Many brands find app-based upsells sufficient for years.

### Is Shopify Plus faster than Advanced?
Platform speed is effectively the same. Speed differences come from theme quality, app bloat and image discipline — fix those before buying any plan upgrade.

### Does Plus improve SEO?
No. Same core platform. SEO performance is driven by architecture, content and speed — plan-independent.

### What revenue justifies Shopify Plus in India?
There's no official threshold, but the practical crossover we observe is around ₹50L–₹1Cr+ monthly online revenue *or* a structural need (multi-store, B2B, heavy drops). Below that, growth spend beats infrastructure spend nearly every time.

## Conclusion

Plan upgrades feel like progress, but revenue comes from traffic, conversion and operations. Run Advanced hard; move to Plus when checkout control, multi-store structure or launch orchestration becomes the binding constraint — and know exactly which feature you're buying when you do.
