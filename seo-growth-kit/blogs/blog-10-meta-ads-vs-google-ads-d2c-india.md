# Meta Ads vs Google Ads for Indian D2C: Where Should Your Next ₹1 Lakh Go?

**Rule of thumb for Indian D2C: Meta usually wins for demand *creation* (visual, impulse, gifting, fashion/beauty under ₹2,000 AOV) and Google wins for demand *capture* (searched categories, higher-consideration purchases, replenishment). With ₹1 lakh to start, we'd typically put it ~70/30 into Meta-led testing with a small branded+Shopping Google layer — then reallocate monthly on contribution margin, not ROAS.**

The honest answer depends on category, AOV and creative capacity. Here's the decision framework, then the exact test plan.

## The core difference in one paragraph

Meta interrupts people who weren't looking for you — it manufactures demand through creative (video, UGC, offers) aimed at lookalike and interest audiences. Google collects people who were already looking — it harvests demand through intent (search terms, Shopping feeds). That's why Meta results depend overwhelmingly on **creative quality and testing velocity**, while Google results depend on **feed quality, keyword architecture and landing pages**. Choose based on which lever you can actually pull well right now.

## Category-by-category guidance (India)

| Your situation | Start with | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Fashion/beauty/lifestyle, AOV ₹500–₹2,500 | Meta | Discovery-led buying; visual creative does the selling; COD-friendly impulse behaviour |
| Supplements/wellness, education-heavy | Meta (creative-led) + Google (problem keywords) | Claims need education; search captures problem-aware buyers |
| Electronics/appliances, AOV ₹3,000+ | Google Shopping + Search | Buyers compare specs; high-intent queries dominate |
| Replenishables (food, consumables) | Meta for acquisition + Google brand/Shopping for retention capture | Subscriptions and repeat purchase amplify both |
| Niche/B2B-ish (pro tools, equipment) | Google Search first | Small but precise monthly search demand; Meta audiences too thin |
| New brand, no search volume for you | Meta | Nobody's searching you yet — you must interrupt to exist |

## The ₹1 lakh test plan (first 30 days)

**₹70,000 — Meta structured test**
1. 1 broad campaign + 1 advantage-style campaign (avoid micro-audience fragmentation)
2. 6–10 creatives: 3 hooks × formats (UGC-style video, static with offer, carousel)
3. Optimize for purchase (or add-to-cart→purchase stairs if volume is thin)
4. Judge on CPA trend and creative-level spend efficiency after ~2 weeks of learning, not daily

**₹20,000 — Google minimum-viable presence**
1. Branded Search (protect your name from marketplace ads — cheap and essential)
2. Shopping feed polished: titles, GTINs, images, price competitiveness
3. 10–15 high-intent non-brand keywords in one tight campaign

**₹10,000 — Reserve for the winner.** Whichever channel shows better contribution margin by day 25 absorbs it next month.

## The metrics trap that ruins these decisions

ROAS alone will mislead you. Meta's assisted role (creating demand people later search for, or buy via COD after multiple touches) is systematically undercounted, and Google's branded sales are often demand Meta created. Decide on **contribution margin per channel per week**, with COD/RTO-adjusted revenue — a 4x ROAS full of COD orders at 30% RTO can be worth less than a 2.2x prepaid-heavy search campaign. This attribution honesty is the difference between scaling and slowly burning.

## When "both, properly" beats either alone

Past roughly ₹3–5L/month in total ad spend, running both is standard for healthy D2C: Meta manufactures demand, Google captures its spillover, branded search protects the brand, and Shopping feeds harvest comparisons. The allocation isn't fixed — we rebalance monthly on margin, seasonality and creative pipeline health.

## What makes Meta work (since that's usually the starting line)

- **Creative velocity:** 4–8 genuinely new concepts a month, not 20 recolors of one ad
- **Hook-first videos:** first 2 seconds decide everything; benefit or pattern-break up front
- **India-native proof:** COD mention, regional language variants, real faces, real packaging
- **Landing-page match:** the ad's promise is the page's headline; slow pages burn creative wins
- **Feed hygiene:** catalogue, events and Conversions API verified — garbage in, garbage optimisation out

## FAQs

### Are Google Ads more expensive than Meta in India?
CPCs on search intent keywords are typically higher than Meta CPMs, but you're paying for demonstrated intent. Judge cost per *acquisition* and contribution margin, never raw CPC — they answer different questions.

### Can a new brand start with Google Ads only?
Only if meaningful search volume exists for your category (electronics, replacements, specific problem queries). If demand must be created, Meta-first is usually unavoidable.

### How long before I know which channel won?
Run a clean 3–4 week structured test, then judge on 30-day contribution margin including COD/RTO adjustments. Daily optimisation of a monthly decision is how brands churn budgets endlessly.

### What about WhatsApp marketing — paid or organic?
It's your retention layer, not a paid-acquisition channel: recovered carts and repeat purchases improve blended margin, which is exactly what makes your ad budget scale further.

### My ROAS dropped this month — should I switch channels?
First check: creative fatigue, seasonality, tracking breakage, COD mix shift. Switching channels to escape a fixable problem is repainting the house because the plumbing leaks.

## Conclusion

Meta creates demand; Google captures it. Start with the one your category and creative capacity favour — for most Indian D2C that's Meta-led — protect your brand terms, and let contribution margin (COD-adjusted) decide the monthly split. The brands that win don't pick a side; they run a disciplined testing system across both.
