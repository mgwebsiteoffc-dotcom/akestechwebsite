# How to Choose a Digital Marketing Agency in Lucknow: A 12-Point Checklist

**Choose an agency the way you'd choose a surgeon: verify outcomes on comparable cases, meet the people who'll actually do the work, and walk away from guarantees. In Lucknow's crowded agency market, the difference between a growth partner and a posting service is visible in the first meeting — if you know what to check.**

This checklist works for any local agency hire; use it as your evaluation scorecard.

## The 12-point evaluation checklist

### Proof & capability (points 1–4)

**1. Case studies with numbers and names.** "200% growth" with no client name, category or baseline is a decoration. Demand: who, what channel, what period, what number — and call one reference if the contract size justifies it.

**2. Results in *your* category.** A great fintech agency may fumble clinic lead economics. Ask specifically: "Show me a clinic/school/builder/ecommerce account you've grown and the cost-per-lead journey."

**3. They can explain *why* past results happened.** Real operators narrate mechanics (creative testing, offer changes, follow-up systems). Script-readers recite dashboards.

**4. Their own marketing is the demo.** An agency's website, Google reviews, GBP presence and content are their own case study. Weak inbound + "we're too busy for our own marketing" is a choice — note it.

### Team & process (points 5–8)

**5. Meet the actual executor, not just the salesperson.** Ask who runs your account day-to-day, their experience, and how many accounts they carry. Ten accounts per strategist is where good intentions go to die.

**6. A written 90-day plan before you pay.** Specific: what gets audited, built, launched and measured in months one to three. Vagueness here predicts the entire relationship.

**7. Reporting you can interrogate.** Ask for a sample monthly report *now*. You're looking for cost per qualified lead, revenue attribution and decisions taken — not impression confetti.

**8. A measurement stack that survives scrutiny.** GA4, call/WhatsApp tracking, CRM-linked lead sources. Agencies that resist tracking do so because tracking ends opinions.

### Commercial hygiene (points 9–12)

**9. Your accounts, your assets.** Ad accounts, GBP, website, CRM, email lists — registered under your ownership with agency access. Never under the agency's roof; you should be able to leave without losing your history.

**10. Contracts with honest exits.** Month-to-month or short lock-ins with clear notice. Confidence shows up as flexibility; 12-month lock-ins with no performance clause are a red flag wearing a tie.

**11. Pricing that maps to scope.** Line-itemed fees (SEO, ads management, creative) vs one mysterious bundle. And ask what's *excluded* — photography, landing pages and ad spend surprises are the classic three.

**12. No forbidden promises.** Guaranteed #1 rankings, "viral" guarantees, guaranteed ROAS before seeing your economics — any of these should end the meeting. Honest agencies commit to process, KPIs and course-correction, not lottery tickets.

## Red flags (instant-walk list)

- "We'll get you on Google's first page in 30 days"
- Refuses to name any client or show any real dashboard
- The proposal is a rate card, not a plan
- All communication happens through one salesperson who changes topic when you ask technical questions
- Wants ad spend paid to the agency's accounts
- Their own Google reviews are absent — or suspiciously uniform

## Green flags (rare and worth paying for)

- Talks about your *unit economics* before tactics (cost per qualified lead, follow-up systems, repeat revenue)
- Shows you a failed campaign and what was learned
- proposes starting with an audit/pilot before a full retainer
- Explains what they *won't* do for your budget
- Their people write/speak publicly in their own name

## The 5 questions that sort agencies in one meeting

1. "Walk me through your last client failure and what changed after it."
2. "What would you do in the first 30 days for a business like mine — specifically?"
3. "How will we know it's working by day 90 — which numbers?"
4. "Who exactly works on my account, and how many others do they carry?"
5. "What does this NOT include?"

Five clear answers = a professional. Evasive answers = a salesperson with your budget.

## FAQs

### How much should a good agency in Lucknow cost?
For meaningful work: roughly ₹15,000–₹75,000/month in fees for local businesses depending on scope (see our cost guide), plus ad spend. Below serious floors you're buying activity, not outcomes — and both cost the same monthly fee until you notice.

### Is a bigger agency always better?
No. Fit beats size: a sharp 15-person specialist team with senior executors beats a 200-person generalist where your account sits in a junior queue. Judge by who touches your account and what they've grown before.

### Should I hire a Lucknow agency or a national/remote one?
Local wins when in-person context matters (real estate, local SEO, community ties, shoots). Remote/national wins for specialised ecommerce ops. Hybrid is common: local strategy partner + specialist execution. Judge by the capability your goal needs, not geography sentiment.

### What deliverables should a standard retainer include?
Audit → fixes → campaigns → content → weekly optimisation → monthly reporting with cost-per-qualified-lead. If the retainer can't name its monthly outputs, you're buying a mood.

### Can I start with a pilot instead of a full contract?
Yes — and strong agencies offer it: a paid audit or 90-day pilot with defined KPIs. It's the lowest-risk way to test the checklist above on real work instead of promises.
