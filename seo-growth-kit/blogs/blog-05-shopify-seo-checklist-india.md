# Shopify SEO in India: The 15-Point Checklist We Run on Every Store

**Shopify SEO in India comes down to 15 things: clean site architecture, keyword-mapped collections and products, fast mobile pages, correct indexing settings, structured data, India-specific content (COD, shipping, sizing), local proof, and a content engine answering real buyer questions.** Most stores do four of these well and none of them completely. Run this list against yours.

## Technical foundations (points 1–5)

### 1. Fix indexing hygiene first
Check `site:yourstore.com` in Google. Remove or noindex what shouldn't rank: internal search results, filtered parameter URLs, policy duplicates. Submit a valid XML sitemap in Search Console — verify it actually loads (we find broken sitemaps on a shocking number of professional stores).

### 2. Kill template bloat before adding anything
Every app injects scripts. Audit monthly: uninstall what's unused, remove leftover theme code, defer non-critical scripts. Mobile LCP over ~2.5–3s on Indian 4G is a conversion leak and a ranking drag. Test on a throttled connection, not your office wifi.

### 3. Structure collections around how Indians search
Shoppers search "kurti sets under 999", "office wear sarees", "protein under ₹1500". Build keyword-named collections and linked landing pages for these demand patterns instead of relying on generic "Shop All" grids. Collection pages are your ranking workhorses — product pages alone can't carry a catalogue.

### 4. Product page on-page standards
- Title: product type + key attribute + brand (e.g., "Cotton Anarkali Kurta Set — Indigo Blue | BrandX")
- Unique descriptions (never paste supplier text — it duplicates across hundreds of stores)
- Alt text on every image describing the product, not "IMG_2042"
- FAQ block on complex products (sizing, fabric, delivery) with FAQ schema

### 5. Structured data that actually validates
Product (price, availability, ratings), BreadcrumbList, Organization. Test in Google's Rich Results Test — plugins lie, validators don't. Eligibility for rich results is free CTR.

## On-page and content (points 6–10)

### 6. One canonical page per intent
If two collections or blog posts target the same query, consolidate them. Cannibalization is the most common reason decent Indian stores plateau at page 2.

### 7. Answer buyer questions on the page
COD availability, delivery time to their city, returns, size help. These are ranking-relevant long-tail queries *and* conversion boosters. An FAQ block serving both jobs is the highest-ROI content on any product page.

### 8. Build the buying-guides layer
"Best fabric for Indian summers", "how to style X", "size guide for Y" — informational clusters that earn links, feed AI answers, and internally link to collections. Two strong guides a month beats ten shallow ones.

### 9. Localise where it matters
Mention India-specific realities in copy: COD, delivery timelines by zone, festive shipping. These phrases match real searches ("cash on delivery kurti online") that generic global stores never answer.

### 10. Fix the blog if you have one
Most store blogs are orphaned: no authors, no internal links, no strategy. Either resource it properly (question-led posts, in-body links to collections, named authors) or stop pretending it exists.

## Authority and local (points 11–15)

### 11. Get your brand entity straight
Same name/address/phone on site footer, Google Business Profile (yes — even pure ecommerce brands benefit for brand searches and service areas), LinkedIn, and marketplaces. AI assistants check these before recommending you.

### 12. Reviews with schema
Product review apps that emit valid `Review`/`AggregateRating` markup. Social proof plus star-rich results plus AI corroboration — one asset, three returns.

### 13. Digital PR in your niche
One placement in a credible fashion/lifestyle/D2C publication beats fifty directory links. Pitch founder story, data ("returns rates we see across 200 stores"), or a strong opinion.

### 14. Marketplace Halo
Amazon/Flipkart listings with strong copy and A+ content rank *inside* marketplaces and often surface in web search for product queries — managed deliberately, they flank your own product pages. Decide which queries you want the marketplace to win (usually none) and differentiate accordingly.

### 15. Measure what matters
Search Console weekly: queries gaining/losing, pages with impressions but weak CTR (rewrite those metas), new question queries appearing (new FAQ/content targets). Rankings are an input; revenue per landing page is the output.

## The 80/20 for stores under ₹25L/month

If you do only five things: **2 (speed), 3 (collection architecture), 4 (product on-page), 7 (buyer-question FAQs), 15 (weekly Search Console review).** Those five compound; everything else is scaling.

## FAQs

### How long does Shopify SEO take to work in India?
Technical and on-page fixes show movement in 4–8 weeks. Collection and content strategy compounds over 3–6 months. Anyone promising page 1 in a fortnight is either targeting zero-volume keywords or lying.

### Is Shopify bad for SEO compared to WordPress?
No — the classic complaints (URL structure, limited technical control) are marginal today. Most Shopify SEO underperformance is content architecture and app bloat, not platform limits.

### Do I need an app for SEO on Shopify?
Useful for schema automation and redirects, but no app substitutes for keyword architecture and content. Avoid "all-in-one SEO" apps that promise everything and slow everything.

### Should Indian stores blog?
Yes — but only question-led, expertise-backed posts that support buying journeys (guides, comparisons, care/how-to). Random trend posts attract the wrong readers and zero revenue.

### What's the #1 SEO mistake you see on Indian Shopify stores?
Duplicate-intent pages: five collections and three blog posts all targeting "cotton kurti set online", each too thin to win. Consolidate, then dominate.

## Conclusion

Shopify SEO in India isn't about tricks — it's a store that loads fast on 4G, is structured around real search demand, answers buyer questions completely, and proves it's trustworthy to both people and machines. Fifteen points, run quarterly, beat any hack.
