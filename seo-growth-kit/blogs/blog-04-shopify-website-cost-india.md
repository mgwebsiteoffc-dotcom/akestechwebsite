# Shopify Website Development Cost in India: An Honest 2026 Pricing Breakdown

**A Shopify store in India typically costs ₹15,000–₹40,000 (freelancer, template-based), ₹40,000–₹2,00,000 (professional agency, customised theme), and ₹2,00,000–₹10,00,000+ (complex custom builds, Shopify Plus, ERP-grade integrations). On top of that, budget for Shopify's subscription (₹1,994–₹24,990/month, billed annually at lower effective rates), apps, and payment gateway fees.**

Those bands are honest market ranges — not quotes. Below is exactly what drives each number, what's included at each level, and where buyers routinely overpay.

## The four real cost layers (most articles mention only one)

1. **Build cost** — designing and developing the store (the part everyone asks about)
2. **Platform cost** — Shopify subscription + apps
3. **Payment/transaction cost** — gateway setup and per-transaction fees
4. **Growth cost** — photography, content, ads, CRO, maintenance (the part that actually decides whether the store earns)

A store that costs ₹60,000 and earns nothing is more expensive than a ₹2,00,000 store that converts from month one.

## The hidden costs buyers discover too late

- **Apps:** ₹0–15,000/month once you add reviews, upsells, WhatsApp, search and loyalty — audit for overlap quarterly
- **Gateway fees:** typically ~2%+ per transaction in India (varies by gateway and plan) — model it into contribution margin
- **Theme licences:** premium themes are usually $180–$400 one-time
- **Rework:** the cheapest quote that ignores tracking and CRO usually gets rebuilt within a year — you pay twice
- **Speed debt:** 15 apps duct-taped onto a slow theme costs conversions every single day

## How to know if a quote is fair (quick test)

1. Does it itemise **catalogue, design, integrations, tracking, QA** separately — or one mysterious number?
2. Does it include **GA4 + Meta pixel + Conversions API** verification before launch? (If not, your ads will fly blind.)
3. Does it include a **redirect map** if you're migrating platforms? (Non-negotiable for SEO.)
4. Is there a written **post-launch support window**?
5. Can they show **two stores live right now** with real traffic?

Three or more "no" answers = you're buying a template install, not a store that sells.

## Where the money is best spent (our honest opinion after 150+ builds)

If budget is limited, the priority order is: **fast premium theme + clean catalogue + bulletproof tracking > custom design > custom functionality > nice-to-have apps.** A beautiful custom store with broken tracking loses to a fast customised theme with perfect data every time. And reserve budget for the first 90 days of traffic — a store without demand generation is a shop in the desert.

## FAQs

### How much does a basic Shopify store cost in India?
A clean, template-based store with up to ~50 products, payments and shipping configured runs roughly ₹15,000–₹40,000 with a freelancer. Quality and post-launch support at the bottom of that range vary wildly — verify live work before paying.

### Why do agencies charge ₹1 lakh+ when freelancers charge ₹20k?
Scope, not markup: catalogue architecture, conversion-focused customisation, analytics verification, integrations, QA across devices, and accountability after launch. If an agency can't articulate that difference, negotiate or walk.

### Is Shopify expensive for Indian sellers?
The Basic plan (₹1,994/month billed annually) plus gateway fees is modest against the revenue a functioning store should produce. The real cost driver is almost always apps and paid traffic, not the platform.

### Can I start with a freelancer and upgrade later?
Yes, and it's often sensible. Just insist on clean fundamentals from day one — standard theme structure, proper tracking, tidy collections — so the upgrade is an enhancement, not a rebuild.

### Does the price include SEO?
Rarely by default. At minimum, demand correct headings, meta control, image alt fields, a sitemap and clean URLs. Growth-level SEO (keyword architecture, content, links) is a separate engagement — and worth budgeting from month one, not year two.

## Conclusion

Shopify development in India costs between roughly ₹15k and ₹10L+ depending on who builds it and how far you take it. Anchor the decision to **what the store must earn**, not the sticker price — and never buy a store that launches without tracking, redirects and a speed budget.
