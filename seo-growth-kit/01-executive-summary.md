# 01 — Executive Summary
**Site:** akestech.com (AKESTECH / Akestech Infotech Pvt. Ltd., Lucknow + Delhi NCR service area)
**Audit date:** 10 Sep 2026 · **Audited via:** repository code, live-site crawls of key templates, public SERP/directory research. Analytics, Search Console, GBP and Instagram logins were **not** available (see Doc 14 for what to connect).

---

## The verdict

AKESTECH has quietly built one of the **strongest SEO foundations of any Shopify-growth agency in North India** — a custom CMS with canonicals, OG/Twitter tags, Organization + LocalBusiness + Service + FAQ + HowTo + Article + Breadcrumb + SoftwareApplication schema, 14 city×service landing pages, 8 industry pages, 19 sub-service pages, 6 free tools/resources, and a 26-post blog.

What is holding it back is **execution debt, not platform debt**:

1. **The sitemap — the site's map for Google — is broken in production** (XML parse error on line 1). Everything else is built on discovery.
2. **Content cannibalization:** three topic clusters each have 2–3 near-duplicate posts (RTO ×3, CRO ×2, "₹5L→₹1Cr scaling" ×2) splitting rankings and confusing both Google and AI engines about the canonical source.
3. **NAP chaos off-site:** at least **4 phone numbers, 3 email addresses, 3 address variants and 2 conflicting domains** (akestech.com vs the stale akestech.in with different branding) are publicly associated with the brand. This is the single biggest local-ranking suppressor.
4. **No named human experts:** every article is authored by "Organization"; the schema logo points to a non-existent `logo.svg`; founders are anonymous. AI engines and Google's E-E-A-T systems both reward named, credentialed people.
5. **Zero verified rankings data** — no Search Console access was available at audit time, so this plan includes a Week-1 measurement setup.

## Current AEO/GEO assessment

- AI engines **can** parse the site (clean HTML, schema on all key templates, answer-style intros on local pages).
- AI engines **can't trust** the site yet: anonymous authorship, conflicting business facts across akestech.com / akestech.in / directories, and no third-party corroboration (no press, few listicles, no named case studies with client names).
- The 26-post blog targets mostly generic global D2C queries where AKESTECH has no authority; the **AEO-worthy differentiating entity content** ("Akestech data reports", India-specific RTO/WhatsApp benchmarks) exists but is buried and duplicated.

## What winning looks like in 90 days

- Sitemap fixed + Search Console fully instrumented → every page indexed.
- 30 new blogs published on **India-specific, low-competition, high-intent** queries (costs, comparisons, how-tos, Lucknow/Delhi NCR local) — all AEO-formatted.
- One canonical post per topic; duplicates merged with 301s.
- NAP unified (one phone, one email, one address format) across site, GBP, Justdial, and akestech.in resolved.
- GBP verified and posting weekly; first 10 new Google reviews.
- Named authors with `Person` schema on every article.
- Target: **+60–100% organic clicks by day 90** (baseline to be set in Week 1 from GSC — currently **Not Available**).
