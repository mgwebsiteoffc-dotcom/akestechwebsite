## 3 · Instagram SEO mechanics

- **Name field keywords:** "Shopify", "D2C", "AI automation", "digital marketing Lucknow" rotate in name/bio
- **Captions:** first line = keyword-bearing hook (searchable); captions indexed by Instagram search — write them for query phrasing ("Shopify speed tips", "WhatsApp automation for stores")
- **On-screen text:** keyphrase in the first frame (OCR-searchable)
- **Alt text:** fill it (advanced settings) with descriptive keywords
- **Hashtags:** 5–10 per post, three tiers — broad (#digitalmarketingindia #d2cindia), niche (#shopifyindia #shopifyagency #whatsappmarketing #performancemarketing), local (#lucknowbusiness #lucknowstartups #delhincrbusiness). Rotate sets; drop dead tags quarterly.
- **Location tags:** Lucknow / Delhi NCR on local posts; collab tags with clients/local partners for reach bleed.

## 5 · The repurposing engine (1 blog → full week of content)

```
1 BLOG → 1 Reel (30–60s: hook = blog's answer block)
       → 1 Carousel (blog's table/checklist, 6–10 slides)
       → 3–5 Stories (poll on the problem → tip slides → link sticker to blog → FAQ box → CTA)
       → 1 GBP post (150-word insight + CTA to blog/audit)
       → 1 LinkedIn post (founder POV angle)
       → cutdowns: 2–3 quote cards for stories; X/Twitter thread if bandwidth
```

Sequencing: publish blog Monday → reel Tuesday → carousel Wednesday → stories through the week → GBP/LinkedIn same week. Batch-produce weekly (one 2-hour shoot + design sprint).

## 6 · Caption & CTA strategy

- Hook (1st line, searchable) → 2–4 short value lines → CTA
- CTA rotation: "Comment [word] for the checklist" (DM automation) · "Link in bio — free audit" · "Save this for your next launch" · "Share with a founder who needs this"
- Every case-study post ends with audit CTA; every educational post ends with save/share CTA (reach signals first, leads second)

## 7 · KPIs (Doc 13 dashboard)
Reach · profile visits · website clicks · saves/shares (weight these — they drive distribution) · follower growth *as a lagging indicator only* · DM/lead count · audit requests attributed to IG (UTM + "where did you hear us" field).

**Effort:** ~8–10 hours/week with batching (or 4–5 with an editor). Tools: Meta Business Suite scheduling + a design template kit; DM automation for keyword triggers.
