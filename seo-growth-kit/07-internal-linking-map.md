# 07 — Internal Linking Map & Topic-Cluster Architecture

**Principle:** every new blog ships with **3–5 in-body contextual links to existing pages** (money pages first) and **2–4 links to sibling blogs**. Every existing page that can pass authority to a new post gets a contextual link within 2 weeks of the post going live. Footer/nav links don't count — in-body contextual links are the currency.

## 5 · Rules of the road

1. **Anchors:** descriptive and varied ("Shopify development cost breakdown", "our WhatsApp automation stack") — never "click here", never exact-match repeated across 5 posts.
2. **Max 1 link per target per article** in-body.
3. **First relevant mention** gets the link; links live in body content, not appended link lists.
4. **Orphan check monthly:** every new post must have ≥2 in-body inlinks from other pages within 14 days of publish.
5. **No-follow nothing internally;** remove any dead links in the quarterly sweep.
