## 1 · Executive summary
AKESTECH's site is a technically modern custom CMS with unusually strong schema coverage, 14 local landing pages, 19 sub-service pages, 8 industry pages, 6 free tools and a 26-post blog — but it is throttled by a **broken production sitemap**, **three content-cannibalization clusters**, **serious NAP/entity conflicts across the web**, **anonymous authorship**, and absence from the third-party listicles and AI-citation sets that define its category. The 90-day plan fixes discovery and trust first, then deploys 30 strategically selected, AEO-formatted blogs and the local/GBP/Instagram engines. **Detail: Doc 01.**
