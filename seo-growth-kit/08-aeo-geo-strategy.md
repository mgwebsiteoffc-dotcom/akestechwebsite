# 08 — AEO/GEO Strategy: Becoming the Source AI Engines Cite

**Goal:** when buyers ask AI systems about Shopify agencies, D2C growth, WhatsApp automation or Lucknow marketing services, AKESTECH is findable, quoteable and recommendable — across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot.

---

## 1 · The three AEO layers (owned site · entity · corroboration)

```
LAYER 3  CORROBORATION   — third parties confirming what you claim (listicles, press, reviews, data citations)
LAYER 2  ENTITY          — who you are, consistent everywhere (NAP, authors, schema, profiles)
LAYER 1  OWNED ANSWERS   — question-shaped, extractable, schema-marked content on akestech.com
```

Most brands attempt layer 3 with no layers 1–2. Sequence matters: answers → entity → corroboration.

## 2 · Layer 1 — Owned answers (content engineering)

**The answer-block standard (applies to every new post and every refreshed old post):**
1. H2 phrased as the exact question buyers ask
2. 40–60 word direct answer immediately after (quotable standalone)
3. Supporting depth below: table (comparisons/prices), numbered steps (processes), definition block ("what is X")
4. FAQ block at end (3–6 questions) with FAQPage schema
5. One stat-free zone rule: never invent numbers; label estimates and internal data with method notes

**Content types mapped to AI surfaces** (the AEO Content Framework):

| Query type | Content format we publish | AI surface targeted | Examples (from the 30) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Informational / definitional | Definition-first guide with glossary block | AI Overviews, PAA, ChatGPT | Blog 1 (AEO), 14 (CAC), merged RTO/COD definitions |
| How-to | Numbered steps + checklist block | AI Overviews (steps), featured snippet | 5, 6, 7, 16, 20, 24, 26, 27 |
| Comparison | 40-word verdict + comparison table | Snippets, AI Overviews, Perplexity | 3, 8, 10, 21, 30 |
| Recommendation ("best") | Criteria-led evaluation incl. competitors | ChatGPT/Perplexity recommendations | 23 (+ existing agency listicle) |
| Local | City cost guides + locality proof + GBP sync | Google AI Overviews (local), Gemini | 22, 24, 25 |
| Data/original research | Annual benchmark reports w/ methodology | Perplexity & ChatGPT citations (rare assets = cited assets) | ROAS benchmarks (refresh annually) |

**Topical authority plan:** the 8 clusters in Doc 07 each get a complete question coverage map (hub + 3–8 supports). Depth per cluster beats breadth across topics — 8 finished clusters read as authority; 40 scattered posts read as noise.

## 4 · Layer 3 — Corroboration (earning the citations)

1. **Listicle land-grab (the single highest-leverage AEO action):** get into the roundups that currently define Lucknow/India agency recommendations — GoodFirms, Sortlist, Clutch, DesignRush, Semrush Agency Partners, plus the self-published "top 10" blogs (outreach with a genuine case study + free audit offer). Target: 8–10 quality placements in 90 days.
2. **Publish our own honest listicle** ("10 best digital marketing agencies in Lucknow 2026 — evaluated transparently, including us") — recommendation-content gets cited; self-promotion doesn't.
3. **Digital PR:** 2 founder-opinion pieces (Shopify/D2C/AI-search angles) pitched to Indian startup/ecommerce publications (YourStory-class, ET BrandEquity-class blogs, TechBullion-class); 1 data story per quarter from the ROAS benchmarks asset.
4. **Review velocity:** Google reviews engine (Doc 09) — review text teaches AI engines what you're *for* ("Shopify", "WhatsApp automation", "Lucknow").
5. **Community presence:** founder activity on LinkedIn/X and relevant subreddits/Quora where D2C questions live — AI engines increasingly weight discussion platforms.
6. **Tool-based links:** pitch the ROAS calculator & speed analyzer to D2C newsletter curators and "free tools" roundups.

## 5 · Measurement (honest taxonomy)

| Metric | Type | Method |
|---|---|---|
| Featured snippet & PAA ownership (question set of ~50) | Measured | Weekly manual/semi-automated checks |
| Google AI Overview presence on priority queries | Measured | Weekly spot-check log (query, date, cited sources) |
| ChatGPT / Perplexity / Gemini mentions | Proxy | Fixed 15-prompt panel, monthly, logged in one sheet |
| Branded search volume trend | Measured | GSC/GTrends — rising brand queries = AI-citation halo |
| Referring domains from listicles/press | Measured | Backlink tool quarterly |
| "Share of AI voice" vs named competitors | Proxy | Same panel prompts, competitor mention count |

Review cadence: weekly snippet/AIO log; monthly full panel; quarterly strategy revision. No vanity "AI rank scores."

## 6 · 90-day AEO milestones

- **Days 1–30:** schema fixes (logo, sitemap), authors live, NAP unified, llms.txt, Bing/BWT set up, answer-block template adopted, Blogs 1–3 live
- **Days 31–60:** 8–10 listicle submissions out, own listicle published, ROAS data report refreshed, 6 more cluster posts live, prompt-panel baseline recorded
- **Days 61–90:** first placement wins logged, AIO/snippet presence on ≥3 target queries (expect, don't promise), entity consistency audit 100% green, quarter-2 data report planned
