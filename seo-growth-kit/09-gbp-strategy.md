## 2 · The 90-day GBP content plan

### Weekly post cadence (13 weeks themed)

| Weeks | Post theme (1–2 posts/week) | CTA |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Launch posts: what AKESTECH does + free audit offer | Book audit |
| 3–4 | Case-study highlights (from /case-studies) with numbers | See results |
| 5–6 | Service spotlights: Shopify development (Lucknow angle) | Get quote |
| 7–8 | Service spotlights: WhatsApp automation + Whatify | Demo |
| 9–10 | Lucknow business content: "marketing cost in Lucknow" insights, local tips | Read guide (→ Blog 22) |
| 11–12 | Offer/lead-magnet posts: ROAS calculator, speed analyzer | Try free tool |
| 13 | Review-compilation + thank-you post | Contact |

Post format: photo/graphic + 150–300 words + one CTA + UTM link. Repurpose from blogs (Doc 10 §5) — 1 blog → 2 GBP posts.

### Photo/video plan
- **Week 1:** 15 photos (office, team, screen work, logos). 
- **Monthly:** 4+ fresh (team moments, new case-study graphics, event/celebration shots). 
- **Quarterly:** 1 office-tour video (60s) + 1 founder explainer.

### Review acquisition engine (the core asset)
1. **Trigger moments:** project milestones, monthly-report delivery, successful launches, campaign wins
2. **The ask (WhatsApp, personal, from the account lead):** *"Hi [Name], glad [result] is live! If you have 2 minutes, a Google review helps a Lucknow agency like ours a lot — here's the direct link: [link]. Thank you!"*
3. **One link, no gating:** same link to everyone; never filter unhappy customers away (policy + ethics)
4. **Target:** 6–10 new reviews/month; every review replied within 48h (thank-you + one specific detail)
5. **Negative reviews:** respond publicly with accountability + resolution path; take detail offline; never argue
6. **Goal state:** 25+ Google reviews at 4.5★+ in 90 days (baseline: verify current count week 1)

### Q&A strategy
Seed 8–10: pricing model, minimum engagement, Shopify specialisation, WhatsApp automation demo, industries served, remote-client process, who owns ad accounts. Answer with specifics + links to blogs.

### Local keyword strategy (what the profile targets)
`digital marketing company lucknow` · `shopify development company lucknow` · `performance marketing agency lucknow` · `meta ads agency lucknow` · `ai automation company lucknow` · `lead generation lucknow` — surfaced via category choice, services, description, posts, photos (file names before upload: `akestech-shopify-lucknow-1.jpg`), and Q&A.

### Local landing-page strategy
GBP website link → homepage (brand searches) or location page (if ads/posts are geo-specific). Every Lucknow-focused post links the matching location page (Doc 07 §4). Track GBP → location-page → contact conversion in GA4.

## 3 · Monthly GBP KPIs (from GBP insights; Doc 13)
Search impressions (branded vs discovery) · map vs search split · calls · direction requests · website clicks · messages · review count/rating trend · photo views.

## 4 · Effort budget
Setup/repair: 1–2 days (week 1) · Ongoing: ~2 hours/week (posts, photos, replies) + review asks embedded in account-team rituals.
